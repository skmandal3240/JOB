## How to move your score

1. **Do the work** from `CURRICULUM.md`, phase by phase.
2. **Push it** to `/projects/<phase>-<name>/` in this repo — code, a README, and results/numbers, not just a description.
3. **Certificates** — drop a note (or the certificate file) in `/certificates/` when you complete one from the list in `SARVAM_AI_APPLICATION_STRATEGY.md`.
4. **External profiles** (GitHub repos elsewhere, Hugging Face Spaces, Kaggle) — paste the links into this file's "External Evidence" section below so they can be reviewed and counted.
5. At each check-in, the tracker gets re-scored against what's actually there, with reasoning, and a log entry is added.
